How an hour is judged

The same method for requests per hour and for the error rate.

Each hour is compared with the same hour of day on the previous {{ anomaly_cfg.baseline_days }} days (09:00 with the last {{ anomaly_cfg.baseline_days }} mornings at 09:00), because traffic always rises and falls through the day.

z = (value − mean of those hours) ÷ standard deviation of those hours
  • Spike: z above +{{ anomaly_cfg.z_threshold }}, far busier than usual.
  • Drop: z below −{{ anomaly_cfg.z_threshold }}, far quieter than usual.
  • Gap: no requests at all. Gap hours are left out of every baseline, so an outage does not make the next week look normal.
  • An hour is only judged when at least {{ anomaly_cfg.min_baseline_points }} earlier days are available; an hour's error rate only when it had at least {{ anomaly_cfg.min_requests }} requests.
Settings: config/pipeline.yaml → anomaly · z_threshold {{ anomaly_cfg.z_threshold }} · baseline_days {{ anomaly_cfg.baseline_days }} · min_baseline_points {{ anomaly_cfg.min_baseline_points }} · min_requests {{ anomaly_cfg.min_requests }}
